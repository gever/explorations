Tactical Field Guide

1. Pack Movement & Fog of War

Command 2 Wolf Scouts (Move 3, Vision 6) and 6 Bears (Move 2, Vision 2).

  • When a unit is selected for movement planning, all other pack members dim slightly so your active unit and reachable hexes stand out.
  • Fog of War (ON by default): Unseen tiles are dark; tiles previously seen by a Wolf or Bear turn neutral gray; active vision shows vibrant colors and spots Trogs. Toggle the Fog button in the top bar at any time to reveal the entire map.

3. Trogs, Grouping & Scaling Hunting AI

Enemies ("Trogs" with clubs 🧌) wander randomly when alone. When Trogs meet, they stick together and actively hunt your nearest Bear or Wolf!

  • Scaling Hunting Efficiency: The larger the Trog group, the more directly and efficiently they track toward your nearest squad member.
  • 1 Bear kills 1–2 Trogs. 3 Trogs together kill 1 Bear.
  • 2 Wolves together kill 1 Trog (a lone Wolf attacking a Trog dies).
  • Coordinated Placement: When multiple squad members defeat a Trog, the first moved unit takes the hex and others slide into adjacent non-water cells.
